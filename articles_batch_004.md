# SCTP Singapore: What the Skills Conversion Programme Means for Workers and Career Changers

**SCTP** stands for the **SkillsFuture Career Transition Programme**, a nationwide train-and-place initiative led by SkillsFuture Singapore (SSG) designed to help mid-career individuals gain industry-relevant skills and move into new sectors or job roles. If you're considering a career switch, facing a shrinking industry, or simply want to stay employable, understanding how SCTP actually works — including its funding, duration, and eligibility — can help you decide whether it fits your situation.

## Key takeaways

- SCTP is a **Train-and-Place** programme: training providers combine skills training with career advisory support and job-search assistance, not just classroom learning.
- Course duration typically ranges from **3 to 12 months**, available in full-time or part-time formats to accommodate different work situations.
- SCTP is open to Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents aged 21 and above (with Long-Term Visit Pass Plus holders eligible for certain courses), covering both employed and unemployed individuals.
- Course fee funding can reach up to **95%** for eligible trainees, and a training allowance of **\$300 to \$3,000 per month** may be available depending on the specific course and provider.
- SCTP courses are delivered by Continuing Education and Training (CET) Centres, including universities, polytechnics, and ITE, and are listed on the MySkillsFuture portal.


## What SCTP actually is

The SkillsFuture Career Transition Programme (SCTP) sits under the broader **SkillsFuture Mid-Career Support Package**, which aims to help locals — particularly those in their 40s and 50s — remain employable and access good jobs as industries evolve. SCTP courses have been progressively rolled out since April 2022, with a target of at least 200 SCTP courses by 2025.

Unlike a typical short course, SCTP is structured as a **Train-and-Place** programme: training providers not only deliver skills training but also provide career advisory services and job-search assistance as part of the course, helping trainees actually land a role in their new sector rather than simply completing a certificate.

## Who can apply for SCTP

SCTP is open to:

- Singapore Citizens and Singapore Permanent Residents aged 21 years and above
- Both **employed and unemployed** individuals, as long as they can commit to the training requirements
- Some SCTP courses also accept Long-Term Visit Pass Plus (LTVP+) holders, depending on the specific training provider and course

There is no restriction limiting SCTP to a specific age band beyond the minimum of 21 — it's designed for anyone looking to transition into a new sector or job role, though it's most commonly used by mid-career professionals.

## How SCTP courses are structured

- **Duration:** typically 3 to 12 months, though individual providers (such as polytechnics) may offer shorter formats of 3 to 6 months for specific courses.
- **Format:** full-time or part-time, giving flexibility for trainees who are still working or have other commitments.
- **Delivery:** a combination of in-person training, online learning, and project work with companies, depending on the course and provider.
- **Assessment:** trainees must meet minimum attendance requirements (commonly at least 75% per module) and pass all associated assessments to remain eligible for funding.


## Funding and financial support under SCTP

SCTP courses come with tiered course fee funding:


| Funding tier | Subsidy level | Who qualifies |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Base Subsidy | Up to 70% of course fee | All eligible Singapore Citizens and PRs |
| Mid-Career Enhanced Subsidy (MCES) | Up to 90% of course fee | Singapore Citizens aged 40 and above |
| Additional Funding Support (AFS) | Up to 95% of course fee | Long-term unemployed (6+ months), ComCare SMTA or Workfare Income Supplement recipients, or Persons with Disabilities |

On top of course fee subsidies, eligible trainees may also receive a **training allowance ranging from \$300 to \$3,000 per month**, depending on the specific course and provider — this is intended to help offset income loss for those training full-time. Trainees can also use their **SkillsFuture Credit** (including the Additional SkillsFuture Credit for Mid-Career Support) to offset any remaining out-of-pocket course fees. Singaporeans under 31 can additionally use their Post-Secondary Education Account (PSEA), and NTUC members can apply their Union Training Assistance Programme (UTAP) benefit, up to \$500 or 50% of course fee, whichever is lower.

## Where SCTP courses are delivered

SCTP courses are offered by **Continuing Education and Training (CET) Centres**, which include:

- Institutes of Higher Learning — universities, polytechnics, and the Institute of Technical Education (ITE)
- NTUC LearningHub and other appointed training providers

Each provider publishes its own list of SCTP course offerings, sector focus, and specific durations. For example, some polytechnics run SCTP courses lasting three to six months with dedicated placement support, including resume coaching and interview preparation, for up to six months after course completion.

## How to find and apply for SCTP courses

1. **Browse the MySkillsFuture portal** (myskillsfuture.gov.sg) and use the course directory, filtering by the "SkillsFuture Career Transition Programme" keyword below the search bar.
2. **Review course details** — sector, duration, format (full-time/part-time), and funding eligibility — for each SCTP course listed.
3. **Apply directly with the training provider**, not through a separate central application system — each CET Centre manages its own applications and interviews.
4. **Confirm your funding eligibility** — base subsidy applies broadly, but MCES and AFS require meeting specific age or circumstance criteria, so check before assuming the maximum subsidy applies to you.

## Is SCTP the right fit for you?

SCTP is best suited if you:

- Are looking to pivot into a **different sector or job role**, not just upgrade skills within your current field
- Can commit to the structured training schedule, including meeting attendance and assessment requirements
- Want **job placement support** built into the training, rather than a standalone course you'd need to leverage yourself
- Qualify for enhanced funding (aged 40+, long-term unemployed, or receiving certain forms of assistance), making the programme significantly more affordable

If you're simply looking to add a skill within your current role without changing sectors, a shorter SkillsFuture-funded course outside SCTP may be more appropriate — SCTP's structure is specifically built around **career transition**, not incremental upskilling.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does SCTP stand for?**
SCTP stands for the SkillsFuture Career Transition Programme, a Train-and-Place initiative under SkillsFuture Singapore's Mid-Career Support Package.

**Who is eligible for SCTP?**
Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents aged 21 and above, whether employed or unemployed, and in some cases Long-Term Visit Pass Plus holders, depending on the specific course.

**How long do SCTP courses take?**
Typically 3 to 12 months, offered in full-time or part-time formats, though some providers offer shorter 3-to-6-month versions of specific courses.

**How much funding support is available for SCTP courses?**
Course fee funding ranges from a 70% base subsidy up to 95% for those who qualify for the Mid-Career Enhanced Subsidy and Additional Funding Support. A training allowance of \$300 to \$3,000 per month may also be available depending on the course.

**Where can I find a list of SCTP courses?**
The MySkillsFuture portal (myskillsfuture.gov.sg) has a searchable course directory — filter using the "SkillsFuture Career Transition Programme" keyword to see current SCTP offerings.

**Can I use my SkillsFuture Credit for SCTP courses?**
Yes. Self-sponsored individuals can use their SkillsFuture Credit and Additional SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career Support) to offset out-of-pocket course fees after funding is applied.

## Final practical takeaway

SCTP is specifically designed for people making a genuine career pivot, not just adding a skill to their current job — the combination of substantial course fee subsidies, a possible training allowance, and built-in job placement support makes it one of the more comprehensive options in Singapore's SkillsFuture ecosystem, provided you're prepared to commit to the structured training and assessment requirements.
